## Entry verification checklist

- [ ] A primary source supports the central factual claim, or the entry is clearly labeled community attributed.
- [ ] Model name, region, and date are scoped correctly.
- [ ] Access date is recorded.
- [ ] Any external download is linked, not mirrored, unless rights are documented.
- [ ] Official image rights are confirmed before any reproduction.
- [ ] The source is linked from the published entry.
- [ ] Conflicts and unknowns are visible in editorial notes.

## Citation format

`Source name — page title — URL — accessed YYYY-MM-DD — evidence level`

Example:

`Official Tamagotchi — History — https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/history/ — accessed 2026-09-18 — Official primary`

## Corrections policy

Proposed: publish a correction log with date, original claim, revised claim, source, and editor. Do not silently overwrite materially incorrect historical or technical information.
