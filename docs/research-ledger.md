## Confidence rules

- Verified: a claim found in a first-party source.
- Corroborated: a claim aligned across distinct attributable sources, pending direct citation in the device record.
- Community record: an attributed fan or forum source; preserve its source and do not present it as official.
- Unverified: a research lead not yet publishable as an index fact.

## Data schema for a device record

Required fields:

- Canonical display name
- Release date and territory
- Platform/generation
- Evidence URL
- Evidence class
- Last checked

Optional but high-value fields:

- Japanese name and romanization
- Regional name
- Shell edition
- Firmware/ROM variation
- Connectivity method
- Battery specification
- Known compatible accessories
- Source-note and unresolved questions

## Rights and image protocol

Do not copy or redistribute franchise imagery, manuals, scans, or promotional assets unless their rights and use permission are verified. The current web build uses original geometric art and typography. Any future image in a TAMA INDEX PDF, document, spreadsheet or page must be either original, licensed, permission-cleared, or embedded under a documented lawful basis.
