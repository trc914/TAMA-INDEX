# TAMA INDEX — Launch Docket

## Editorial proposition

TAMA INDEX is an independent, research-based Tamagotchi wiki designed as a living reference work. Its visual language is authored through CUT STATE: typographic, precise, tactile, and editorial. CUT STATE is the foundry and visual system; TAMA INDEX is the Tamagotchi archive.

The public experience should feel like a carefully edited cultural index rather than a fan-image feed, ecommerce site, or generic AI-generated encyclopedia.

## Course topography

The archive is organized by four interpretive strata rather than a flat list:

- Origin / 1996–2003: original devices, regional variants, early character and care systems
- Connection / 2004–2010: infrared exchange, networks, family mechanics, color-screen transition
- Platform / 2011–2020: iD, 4U, m!x, Meets/On and regional digital ecosystems
- Contemporary / 2021–present: Pix, Smart, Uni, Paradise, anniversary reissues, live service and download eras

This is an editorial navigation model, not a claim that every model within a period shares identical mechanics.

## Visual direction

### Design principles

- Use typography, grids, paper-like field color, ruled lines, and restrained signal colors before photography.
- Use original diagrams and data marks to make mechanics and chronology legible.
- Use generous empty space; treat asset placement as editorial evidence, not decoration.
- Keep pages source-forward. A citation is a visible component, not a footnote afterthought.
- Avoid faux-handwritten fonts, neon kawaii overload, stock 3D devices, mood-board collage, and repeated AI motifs.

## Competitive observation

### Tama-Palace

Tama-Palace presents a long-running fan-news model founded in 2006 and relaunched on Tumblr in 2015. Its apparent strength is velocity: timely community and product-update coverage. TAMA INDEX should not duplicate its feed format; it should link to relevant reporting where appropriate and differentiate through source provenance, stable entry pages, and research-grade navigation.

### Gotchi Garden

Gotchi Garden is an established guide-oriented blog with model-specific pages, care materials, charts, and links to other community resources. Its apparent strength is practical guide depth. TAMA INDEX should differentiate with normalized metadata, explicit regional/version scope, citation quality, original explanatory graphics, and a cohesive editorial interface.

Research evidence: Search results and linked public pages, reviewed September 18, 2026. Page-level claims require individual verification before publication.

## Research and data protocol

### Evidence levels

- Official primary: official Tamagotchi, Bandai, manuals, press releases, product pages
- Official archived: an official historical page or archived official material; flag if current availability is uncertain
- Community attributed: a named fan resource with a link and clear attribution
- Unverified: a lead only; never publish as a definitive fact

### Required record fields

Every entry must include title, device family, release year, release region, original model name where known, source URL, source type, access date, verification status, rights status, and editorial notes.

### Publication rule

Do not treat a secondary fan guide as confirmation of regional release details, technical behavior, or downloadable files. Use it as an attributed reference or locate a primary source.
